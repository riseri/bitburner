# Unattended BN10.1–BN10.3

Enter BN10, sync all of `src`, and run `supervisor.js` on home. If supervision is
already running, use `supervisor-restart.js` to load the new policy. The intended
setup has SF1.3, SF2, SF4.3 and SF5; BN10 itself supplies sleeve access before
you own SF10. Earlier SF4 levels have higher script RAM costs.

The route clears BN10.1 and BN10.2 using `bootstrap.js` to restore supervision.
At the final BN10.3 clear it calls `destroyW0r1dD43m0n(10)` **without a callback**.
This awards SF10.3 and enters a fresh BN10 with all scripts stopped. The game
awards Source-Files on entering a node, so stopping at the BitVerse selection
screen would not yet award SF10.3. With SF10.3 already owned, the route authorizes
no further automatic transition. Other BitNodes keep their existing route.

## Sleeves and gang access

`sleeve-manager.js` is enabled by default (`--sleeves true`), runs on home, and
publishes authenticated status on port 2. It reads the actual sleeve count on
each pass, so the extra sleeves from subsequent clears need no configuration.
Its default policy takes control of sleeve tasks; set `--sleeves false` to manage
them yourself.

- Before gang access, sleeves synchronize to 10, then select crimes by expected
  successful karma per second. They do not wait for 100 synchronization or zero
  shock. Shock affects income/XP, while crime karma depends on success and sync.
- The augmentation manager also uses owned player Homicide work for one minute
  in five, plus cash/passive-reputation waits. Normal faction work gets the other
  four minutes. Ready purchases and approved installs take priority, and unrelated
  manual player work is preserved. `--gang-karma false` disables both player and
  sleeve karma policy; `--augmentation-work false` disables player work only.
- At -54,000 karma, the gang manager creates a gang once NiteSec or The Black Hand
  is joined. Existing backdoor/join automation supplies those invitations. Karma
  and the gang survive augmentation installs; each new BitNode needs a new gang.
- After karma, the most ready sleeve can help the selected faction or study free
  Computer Science for a hacking milestone. Other sleeves synchronize, recover
  shock, then earn crime income. Only one sleeve works a faction; the gang faction
  is excluded. Identical tasks are left running rather than restarted every tick.
  Rejected tasks fall back to crime and retry after a minute.

Gang creation is a parallel benefit, **not a gate on augmentation progression**.
BN10 still follows the normal Daedalus/Red Pill path. The dashboard shows sleeve
tasks, synchronization, shock, memory, karma, and estimated sleeve karma rate.
Karma and sleeve recovery can legitimately take hours; these waits do not stop
the hacking engine or augmentation loop.

## Spending and bootstrap

`sleeve-upgrades.js` is a separate optional helper. At most once a minute it can
spend up to `--sleeve-budget 0.02` of surplus cash, after the shared savings floor.
It buys at most one item: an affordable Covenant sleeve, a memory point, or a
shock-free sleeve augmentation. Covenant purchases are BN10-only and require
existing membership. It does not save for them, force a Covenant detour, or hold
the final clear for all five purchasable sleeves. Use budget `0` to disable buys;
the maximum accepted budget is `0.1`.

The helper waits for spare RAM beyond the home reserve, and both tasks and buys
pause for an authenticated pending augmentation installation. Its cost is not
part of the critical helper reserve. Contract scans can borrow spare RAM while
sleeves, gangs or augmentation management wait for a home upgrade. Progression
actors retain their reserve and funded home upgrades admit the blocked services.

The installed 3.0.1 game's RAM calculator, with SF4.3, reports 8.20 GB for the
supervisor, 42.55 GB for sleeve management, and 12.05 GB for the largest progression
actor. Those fit together in 64 GB once core controllers are remote. Adding gang
and augmentation management (including grafting) needs 229.30 GB including that actor reserve, fitting
256 GB. Other enabled services may require additional RAM.

## Validation

Focused tests cover sleeve task continuity, crime choice, bounded player work,
manual-work preservation, purchases/resets during karma farming, stale/reset
status, spending guards, failed task recovery, service admission, bootstrap
settings and all three clears. The final clear is asserted to award SF10.3 with
no bootstrap callback. Existing endgame tests cover bursty XP and beneficial
augmentation resets instead of indefinite unknown-ETA waits.

`test/installed-game-ram.test.cjs` optionally loads the installed game's source
map and runs its actual RAM calculator against the repo. It skips if that source
map or Node's TypeScript stripping is unavailable; `BITBURNER_SOURCE_MAP` can
select another installation. These are local automated tests, not a claim that
three live BitNodes have been completed during development.

## Grafting

`--grafting true` is the default on the supervisor and augmentation manager. New
automatic grafts are restricted to BN10 and automatic reset policy. Explicit
augmentation targets and disabling route, player work or purchasing disable new
grafts. `--grafting false` opts out; already-running grafts still finish safely.

The manager compares live graft price and duration with continuing toward the
current hacking/reputation milestone, normal acquisition plus reset recovery,
and the endgame package/reset quote. It requires at least 20% and 60 seconds of
estimated improvement. Hacking requires fresh measured XP and skill Formulas;
reputation uses the faction planner's available work rate. Unknown normal
acquisition is treated optimistically as immediate, not as an infinite wait.
Missing recovery uses an optimistic normal-install comparison with no recovery
delay. These estimates are deliberately conservative, not a globally optimal
schedule or a promise of a particular node completion time.

The comparison includes the unfocused-work penalty, travel cost, full capital
replacement time, lost player work during grafting, and the next 2% entropy
penalty on affected skills, XP, reputation, income and other multipliers.
Congruity's entropy removal/immunity is modeled. No background XP or cash earned
during the graft is credited to its projected finish time. Grafts require
installed prerequisites and cash above shared savings, the percentage reserve
and current milestone cash. They never create a savings hold.

Active grafts survive manager restarts and are protected from resets, karma,
travel and faction/class work, including grafts started manually. Completion is
confirmed through installed ownership; interruption resumes normal progression
with a five-minute retry delay. Quote errors retry once per minute, and failed
starts retry after five minutes while normal progression continues. The manager
does not wait on the graft's long-running Promise. The dashboard shows its
decision, cost, work duration, resulting entropy, and comparable milestone ETAs.

Grafting adds 20 GB to augmentation management (134.90 GB with SF4.3 in 3.0.1).
It uses the manager directly, so there is no separate helper RAM reservation.
Bootstrap tests cover the updated cost and automatic admission after the 256 GB
home upgrade. Grafting tests cover six simulated hours of unavailable quotes,
active work protection, completion, interruption, failed starts, stale XP,
entropy, funding/price changes, and configuration opt-outs.
